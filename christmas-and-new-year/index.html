FAQ

What is in the pack?

The work do, Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year’s Eve and winter. 300+ cards across Mild, Cheeky and Get Fu**ed.

Do the cards change with the date?

Yes. In November the work do comes first, around Christmas Day mostly Christmas, and towards midnight on the 31st mostly Boxing Day and New Year.

What does it cost?

It is a one-time purchase. Try 5 cards for free first, and Original has 50 free cards every night.
